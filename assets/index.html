    Awareness

    While Heron is open, it quietly watches your working folder so it already knows what you are in the middle of. It sees file names and times, never what is inside, and never passwords or key files. Everything it notices stays on this computer.

    How much Heron does on its own

    Suggesting, preparing and taking over send what Heron noticed (file names, your tasks and what it knows about you) to your AI model about every half hour while there is new activity, which uses tokens.
