Life in a tide pool

A tide pool is a rocky pocket of seawater left behind when the tide goes out. Animals that live there, such as anemones, limpets and small crabs, must cope with sudden swings in temperature and salt.

Many of them hold tight to the rock or close their shells while the pool is exposed, and feed again once the next tide returns.
